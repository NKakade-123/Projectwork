GLOBAL INSERT COMPATIBILITY

Brands We Deal In & Insert Solutions

Mamta Tools manufactures precision tool holders and bodies fully compatible with world-standard carbide insert geometries for turning, milling, drilling, and boring.

1. Turning Inserts

CNMG, WNMG, TNMG, DNMG, VNMG in multiple chipbreaker geometries for roughing to fine turning.

2. Threading Inserts

Metric, UN, Whitworth, NPT, and trapezoidal thread profiling inserts with multi-layer PVD/CVD coatings.

3. U Drill Inserts

WCMX, SPMX, and SOMT high-efficiency inserts for rapid holemaking in steel, stainless, and cast iron.

4. Milling Inserts

APMT, APKT, SEKT, RPMT, RDMT, and high-feed insert configurations engineered for high metal removal.

5. Boring Inserts

CCMT, CPMT, TCMT, and micro-boring geometries delivering sub-micron bore surface tolerances.

6. Grooving Inserts

Parting-off and deep grooving inserts with reinforced clamping to withstand high radial cutting loads.
