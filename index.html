Welcome to Mamta Tools India's Benchmark in Precision Tooling

Established in 2004 under the inspiring leadership and technical guidance of Mr. A. M. Ghagare, Mamta Tools has grown into one of India's most respected manufacturers, suppliers, and exporters of world-class CNC and VMC indexable cutting tools.

Our manufacturing facility situated in Talawade, Pune, utilizes state-of-the-art CNC tool grinding, high-precision CAD/CAM tool modeling, and rigid metallurgical testing to produce cutting tools that minimize cycle time, eliminate chatter, and dramatically reduce cost-per-component.
