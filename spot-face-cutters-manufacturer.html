Spot Face Cutters

We are Manufacturer, Supplier, Trader, Exporter of Cutting Tools, Spot Face Cutters For Center Cutting, For Regular Cutting; and our setup is situated in Talawade, Pune, Maharashtra, India.

Spot Face Cutters are specialized tools used in machining to create flat surfaces on workpieces with precision and accuracy. These cutters are designed for specific applications where a flat, level surface is required, often for accommodating bolts, screws, or other fasteners. The distinctive feature of Spot Face Cutters is their flat-bottomed design, which allows them to remove material only from a targeted area without affecting the surrounding regions.

This precision makes them ideal for tasks like preparing seating surfaces for fasteners, ensuring a flush and secure fit. The cutting edges of Spot Face Cutters are engineered for efficiency and consistency, enabling the creation of smooth, uniform flat spots on a variety of materials. Industries such as manufacturing, automotive, and aerospace frequently rely on Spot Face Cutters to achieve the necessary surface finish and dimensional accuracy in critical components. These cutters play a crucial role in enhancing the quality and reliability of machined parts by facilitating the creation of well-defined flat surfaces in a controlled and efficient manner.

Some of the advantages of using Spot Facing Cutter:

  • Spot facing cutter are use for milling spot faces of blind and countersunk holes.
  • The Spot Face Cutters can be availed in numerous spot face diameters, which ensures its versatility.
  • Simple handling in setting and insert replacement.
  • Excellent performance in open and closed slotting, edging, face cavity milling process.

ENGINEERING OVERVIEW

True Flat Seating Surfaces & High Perpendicularity

Spot facing operations require strict perpendicularity relative to the hole axis to ensure that washer and bolt seating surfaces distribute clamping pressure evenly without bending stresses. Mamta Tools spot face cutters feature rigid hardened tool bodies with radial and axial runouts held within 0.005 mm, preventing chatter marks and delivering uniform Ra 0.8 mirror finishes on uneven rough castings.
