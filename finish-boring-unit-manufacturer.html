Finish Boring Units, Adjustable Finish Boring Units

We are Manufacturer, Supplier, Trader, Exporter of Finish Boring Units, Adjustable Finish Boring Units, Boring Unit Extensions, Rough Boring Units, Adjustable Rough Boring Units, Cutting Tools and our setup is situated in Talawade, Pune, Maharashtra, India.

Finish Boring Units, Adjustable Finish Boring Units are specialized machining tools designed for the final stages of boring operations, where the emphasis is on achieving tight tolerances, smooth surface finishes, and precise dimensions. These units play a critical role in refining the dimensions of pre-machined holes, ensuring that the end product meets the desired specifications. Finish boring involves the removal of minimal material to achieve the required accuracy and surface quality.

These units are equipped with cutting tools and mechanisms that prioritize precision, minimizing any deviation from the intended specifications. Commonly used in industries such as aerospace, automotive, and manufacturing, Finish Boring Units, Adjustable Finish Boring Units contribute significantly to the production of components where meticulous attention to detail is crucial. The precise control over dimensions and surface finishes provided by Finish Boring Units, Adjustable Finish Boring Units is essential for creating components that meet stringent quality standards, making them indispensable in applications where precision is paramount.

Some of the advantages of using Finish Boring Units:

  • Finish boring unit are performed to complete an existing hole and to achieve a close hole tolerance and correct positioning with high quality surface finish.
  • Finishing hole with tight tolerances requires tools that can be adjusted for accuracy in small measures-a capability that fine boring heads readily offer.
  • Precision Finish Boring Bar with high accuracy & repeatability.
  • Facilitates precision adjustment with least count of 1 micron (0.001mm) radially.

ENGINEERING OVERVIEW

Zero-Backlash Lead Screw & 1-Micron Radial Control

Precision bearing bores, hydraulic valve spools, and automotive engine crank bores mandate dimensional accuracies within fractions of a millimeter. Mamta Tools fine finish boring units incorporate hardened, pre-loaded ground lead screws that eliminate backlash during setting. The direct reading dial allows CNC operators to expand or retract the insert tip by 0.001 mm directly on the machine spindle without removing the tool holder.
