Ruffer Cutters

We are Manufacturer, Supplier, Trader, Exporter of Cutting Tools, Ruffer Cutters and our setup is situated in Talawade, Pune, Maharashtra, India.

Ruffer Cutters are specialized tools designed for precision cutting in various applications, ranging from construction and metalworking to woodworking. These cutting instruments are equipped with rugged blades that enable users to achieve clean and accurate cuts with ease. The term "Ruffer" suggests a robust and durable construction, emphasizing the tools' ability to tackle tough materials with efficiency.

These cutters typically feature ergonomic designs, allowing for comfortable handling during prolonged use. Whether it's trimming, shaping, or slicing through different materials, Ruffer Cutters are engineered to provide users with the precision and reliability needed for a wide array of cutting tasks. Their versatility makes them a valuable asset in the toolkit of professionals and DIY enthusiasts alike, offering a dependable solution for achieving clean and efficient cuts across diverse projects.

Some of the advantages of using Ruffer cutter:

  • Ruffer cutter specially use for rough machining.
  • Ruffer cutter are used for roughing cut on component and they are also preferred to use inside holes there you need chip evacuation better and doing a finish cut later.
  • High insert thickness and strong obtuse corner angle improve stability, contributing to excellent system reliability.
  • Ruffer cutter is also use for unstable work pieces.

ENGINEERING OVERVIEW

Interrupted Cuts, Raw Castings & Severe Stock Removal

Machining unstable workpieces and raw casting crusts presents severe shock loads that quickly destroy conventional tooling. Our ruffer cutters utilize negative-positive geometries with reinforced obtuse insert cutting corners and thick insert cross-sections. This channels high mechanical impact inward toward the tool center rather than the fragile edge, providing reliable roughing without edge chipping or catastrophic tool failure.
