ENGINEERING OVERVIEW

Dynamic Balancing & Runout Control for Spindle Protection

Poor-quality collets and imbalanced tool holders cause excessive spindle bearing wear, erratic tool deflection, and premature insert fracture. Mamta Tools accessories undergo cryogenic treatment and precision grinding to guarantee total indicator runout under 5 microns. All tool holders are dynamically balanced to G2.5 at 20,000 RPM, delivering stable cutting performance and extending machine spindle service life.
